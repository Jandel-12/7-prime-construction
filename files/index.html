ABOUT US

Z PRIME CONSTRUCK is a construction support services company specializing in hauling, aggregates delivery, heavy equipment rental, excavation, site preparation, and construction subcontracting.

We work behind and beside the builders, so the dirt, the materials and the machines are never the reason a project slows down.

  • Reliability We show up on schedule and finish what we start.
  • Safety Every load and every operation is run with care.
  • Efficiency Right equipment, right crew, no wasted hours.
  • Professionalism Clear communication from quote to completion.
  • Quality service Work we are comfortable putting our name on.
